How We Roast
We started Deschutes Trail Coffee in 2019 with a single 12-kilo drum roaster and a weekend stand at the Bend Farmers Market. Every lot is cupped in-house and packaged within 24 hours of roasting.
Sourcing Standards
We buy direct-trade green coffee from cooperative farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, publishing farm-gate pricing each harvest season.
