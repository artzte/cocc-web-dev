## Week 1 Quiz: Modern Dev Workflow & Semantic Foundations

### Instructions

Answer each of the following five questions in a short essay response. Explain the concepts in your
own words, and feel free to include brief examples.

---

### Question 1: Semantic HTML vs. Generic `<div>` Tags

As discussed in [web.dev Learn HTML: Semantic HTML](https://web.dev/learn/html/semantic-html), why
is it better to use semantic landmark elements like `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, and `<footer>`
instead of styling generic `<div>` elements to look the same?

---

### Question 2: Heading Hierarchy

Based on
[web.dev Learn HTML: Headings and Sections](https://web.dev/learn/html/headings-and-sections),
explain why you should choose heading tags (`<h1>` through `<h6>`) based on document outline rather
than visual font size.

---

### Question 3: `<section>` vs. `<article>`

Compare `<section>` and `<article>` as described in
[web.dev Learn HTML: Semantic HTML](https://web.dev/learn/html/semantic-html) and give one concrete
example of content that belongs in `<article>`.

---

### Question 4: Why `<html lang="en">` and the Viewport `<meta>` Tag Matter

In [web.dev Learn HTML: Document Structure](https://web.dev/learn/html/document-structure), the
document skeleton includes `<html lang="en">` and `<meta name="viewport">`. Pick **one** of those
two pieces of markup and explain what goes wrong for users when it is omitted.

---

### Question 5: Git/PR Workflow vs. FTP

In this course, we use Git branches, Pull Requests, and automated deployment instead of uploading
files manually over FTP. Explain one practical advantage of a Git and Pull Request workflow when
maintaining a real business website.
